What it left behind

Habits that turned out to transfer directly into technical work.

  1. 01

    Checklists over memory

    Procedures exist because people are unreliable under pressure and at the end of a long day. A written cutover plan is the same instinct applied to a migration.

  2. 02

    Brief before, debrief after

    Say what is going to happen, then afterwards say honestly what actually happened and what would change next time. Most organisations do the first half and skip the second.

  3. 03

    Maintain before it fails

    Scheduled maintenance is cheaper than unscheduled repair, in equipment and in infrastructure. It is also the difference between a quiet week and a bad one.

  4. 04

    Own the outcome

    Report the situation accurately including the parts that reflect badly on you, because the alternative costs someone else time they do not have.
