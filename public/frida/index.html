Houston, Texas · July 13

Frida Chip

Somewhere to keep a person I love - in photographs, music, dumb jokes, little pieces of time, and whatever else deserves not to disappear.

Why this page exists

Somewhere between Houston and San Diego, you became part of the rhythm of my life.

There have been flights, an SDFC game, days together that always seem to go by too quickly, and a collage made from eighty photographs I traced by hand because apparently printing them normally would have been too easy. Moonlight Beach is still waiting for us.

I think this page comes from the same instinct as that collage - wanting to keep the little things, give them somewhere to live, and make something for you that could only ever really belong to you.

Pluckers

Pluckers is your favorite bar in Houston. Wings, a Trashcan, and somewhere to decompress after a long shift at HEB - especially when Brenda is there with you.

I've never needed a Houston travel guide. At this point I could probably find Pluckers entirely from hearing you talk about it.

Houston

Houston, because of you

Houston used to be a place on a map to me. Now it has landmarks.

HEB. Pluckers. The roads you drive. The places you go after work. The people you spend your time with. The airport I associate with seeing you and leaving you.

It's strange how knowing one person can slowly give an entire city meaning.

Written down

Little things for Frida

Space City

You come from Space City,
which feels a little too appropriate.

Because somehow a person
several states away,
and one obnoxiously long flight away,
became someone I carry everywhere.

Houston can keep the rockets.

I found what I was looking for.

California trees

California has redwoods
old enough to have watched
entire lifetimes come and go.

Trees taller than buildings,
roots underneath places
people haven't even named yet.

And still -

if somebody asked me what I would cross
all that distance to see,
it would be you.

Between here and Houston

There are highways, airports,
time zones, departures, arrivals,
plans made weeks ahead
for days that disappear too quickly.

And somehow none of that
has ever made you feel temporary.

You became part of my life from far away.
I think that's beautiful.

Proud

I am proud of you.

Not only when something goes right.
Not only when there is something obvious
to celebrate.

I am proud of the woman you are,
the woman you are becoming,
the life you are building,
and the way you keep becoming
more yourself.

I love you for more reasons
than I could fit on this page.

Houston → San Diego.
San Diego → Houston.

And then?

And then Houston → San Diego again. And then San Diego → Houston again. And then a page on the internet that nobody asked for. Dude, you got a website.

I made a page for you because apparently simply missing you like a normal person wasn't enough.
