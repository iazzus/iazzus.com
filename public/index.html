Men's Physique

Built to a standard, not a mood

Men's Physique rewards a specific shape: wide shoulders, a developed chest and arms, a back with both width and thickness, and a waist that keeps the proportion honest. That is the target, and everything in the program is judged against it.

Training is planned in blocks, logged every session, and adjusted on evidence rather than enthusiasm.
