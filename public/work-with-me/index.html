Process

How it works

  1. 01

    Tell me the situation

    Describe the problem as it actually is rather than the solution you think you need. A short message is enough to start.

  2. 02

    A straight answer

    Whether this is something I can help with, roughly what it involves, and what it would cost. If it is not a good fit I will say so rather than take the work.

  3. 03

    Agreed scope

    What is included, what is not, what success looks like and when it is finished - written down before anything starts, so there is nothing to argue about later.

  4. 04

    Delivery and handover

    The work, then documentation you can actually use. For training, that means a plan you understand well enough to run without me.
