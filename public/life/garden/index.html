Life

Garden

The slowest feedback loop I work in. A mistake in a training block shows up in six weeks; a mistake in the garden shows up next season. It is a useful corrective to a job where everything is expected immediately.

Approach

Soil first, then patience

Most garden problems are soil problems wearing a costume. Feed the soil, water deeply and less often so roots go down instead of sideways, and resist the urge to intervene every time a leaf looks wrong.

Planting goes in on a plan rather than on impulse at the garden centre, and what worked and what failed gets written down at the end of a season — because by the following spring nobody remembers accurately.
