Life

Family

The fixed point everything else is scheduled around. Training happens early because of it, work gets finished because of it, and most of the good photographs on this site are going to come from it.

Why it is here

Present, without being published

A personal site that leaves out the most important part of a life is not really a personal site. But the people in these photographs did not choose to have a public profile, and anything put on the internet with a name attached to it stays there and stays searchable.

So this page keeps photographs and leaves out the rest. No names, no ages, no schools, no locations, no daily routines. That is enough to show what matters without building a record about people who never asked for one.

Before you publish

Strip EXIF data from every photograph first — phone cameras embed GPS coordinates, and a single geotagged image undoes the whole point of leaving the location out. There is a one-line command for it in assets/images/README.md.
